Shot 1.0 is the first stable release of Shot, a free menu bar app for screenshots, annotation, screen recording and GIFs on macOS 15 and later. Here is what came in during the beta.

### Highlights

- Every window is redesigned, the gallery has its own window, and Copy is the main button in both editors
- Scrolling capture stitches a long page or chat thread into one screenshot while you scroll
- The gallery has folders and tags, and its search finds the text inside your screenshots
- Shot is translated into eight languages and follows the language your Mac uses
- The capture bar, both editors, the video timeline and the gallery work from the keyboard and with VoiceOver
