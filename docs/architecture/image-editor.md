# Image editor

Read this before changing annotations, their rendering or the editor's undo.

- `EditorDocument` / `Annotation` (ShotCore) is the model.
- `AnnotationRenderer` is shared by the on-screen canvas, flattened export, and the video compositors (`ProjectCompositor` via `AnnotationFrame`/`RenderCache`), so a rendering change affects them all.
- Undo is snapshot-based through ShotCore's generic `UndoStack`, not `NSUndoManager`. Group continuous edits (slider drags, held keys) into one step. View state like zoom is not undoable.
- Annotation outlines are `CGPath`s, hit-tested by Core Graphics. Filters are `CoreImageEffects`, text is `CoreTextShaper`, and OCR is Vision.
- `EditorModel` keeps view state and talks to the canvas. The undo steps, coalescing and unsaved-changes tracking are ShotCore's `EditHistory`, and what the crop tool applies is decided by `CropEditing`.
- The window's title bar is `EditorTitleBar` in `EditorRootView.swift`: the file name and size, the tool group (`EditorToolGroup`), Undo, Style, Layers, the Share menu, Save and Copy. ShotCore's `EditorToolbarLayout` says which tools have a button and in what order, and `EditorShareAction` holds the Share menu's order and which of its actions save first. `EditorModel.share(_:)` runs them. The selected tool's options are the style bar over the canvas (`StyleBar.swift`): `StyleBarOverlay` places it with ShotCore's `StyleBarPlacement` beside `EditorModel.styleBarMark`, which the canvas keeps current in view points, and `StyleBarContent` says what it holds. More style, its popover, has the options that don't fit and Reset. The option views in `EditorToolbar.swift` (`ShapeMenu`, `RedactionOptions`, `SpotlightOptions` and the rest) are now used only by the video editor's palette.
- Along the canvas's foot, `CanvasFoot` in `CanvasControls.swift` holds the zoom control, the Canvas menu and the hint. ShotCore's `CanvasHint` picks the hint from the tool, the selection and whether text is being typed, and `ZoomControl` gives the percentage and when − and + can step. `StyleBarPlacement` keeps the style bar clear of the foot through `EditorModel.canvasFootHeight`. `CanvasHost` wraps the canvas in `EditorCanvasContainer`, whose backdrop view draws the dotted background and the image's shadow on a layer of its own, so a drag never redraws the blur.
