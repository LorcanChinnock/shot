# Image editor

Read this before changing annotations, their rendering or the editor's undo.

- `EditorDocument` / `Annotation` (ShotCore) is the model.
- `AnnotationRenderer` is shared by the on-screen canvas, flattened export, and the video compositors (`ProjectCompositor` via `AnnotationFrame`/`RenderCache`), so a rendering change affects them all.
- Undo is snapshot-based through ShotCore's generic `UndoStack`, not `NSUndoManager`. Group continuous edits (slider drags, held keys) into one step. View state like zoom is not undoable.
- Annotation outlines are `CGPath`s, hit-tested by Core Graphics. Filters are `CoreImageEffects`, text is `CoreTextShaper`, and OCR is Vision.
- `EditorModel` keeps view state and talks to the canvas. The undo steps, coalescing and unsaved-changes tracking are ShotCore's `EditHistory`, and what the crop tool applies is decided by `CropEditing`.
- The window's title bar is `EditorTitleBar` in `EditorRootView.swift`: the file name and size, the tool group (`EditorToolGroup`), Undo, Style, Layers, the Share menu, Save and Copy. ShotCore's `EditorToolbarLayout` says which tools have a button and in what order, and `EditorShareAction` holds the Share menu's order and which of its actions save first. `EditorModel.share(_:)` runs them. The selected tool's options are the style bar over the canvas (`StyleBar.swift`), except in crop mode. `StyleBarOverlay` places it with ShotCore's `StyleBarPlacement` beside `EditorModel.styleBarMark`, which the canvas keeps current in view points, and `StyleBarContent` says what it holds. More style, its popover, has the options that don't fit and Reset. The option views in `EditorToolbar.swift` (`ShapeMenu`, `RedactionOptions`, `SpotlightOptions` and the rest) are now used only by the video editor's palette.
- Crop mode has the crop bar (`CropBar.swift`) at the canvas's foot in place of the style bar and the hint: Aspect, the size the crop keeps, Reset, Cancel and Crop. ShotCore's `CropBar` places it clear of the zoom control and gives the size, and `EditorModel.cropBarClearance` tells the canvas how much of its bottom the bar covers, so the fitted image and the crop's bottom handles stay above it. While cropping, Copy in the title bar steps down to a secondary button, since Crop is the next step. The canvas draws the crop itself (`drawCrop`), and `CropEditing` decides what applying it changes.
- Along the canvas's foot, `CanvasFoot` in `CanvasControls.swift` holds the zoom control, the Canvas menu and the hint. ShotCore's `CanvasHint` picks the hint from the tool, the selection and whether text is being typed, and `ZoomControl` gives the percentage and when − and + can step. `StyleBarPlacement` keeps the style bar clear of the foot through `EditorModel.canvasFootHeight`. `CanvasHost` wraps the canvas in `EditorCanvasContainer`, whose backdrop view draws the dotted background and the image's shadow on a layer of its own, so a drag never redraws the blur.
- The window is the title bar over a hairline, then the canvas edge to edge with the open side panel flush against its right. Both panels draw their surface with `sidePanel(width:)` (`Design/SidePanel.swift`). The Layers panel (`Design/LayersPanel.swift`) lists `EditorModel.layerRows`, frontmost first, with `frameRow` pinned above them while there's a crop; ShotCore's `FrameRowDetail` says what the Frame row shows on its right.
- The Style panel (`StylePanel.swift`) sits beside the canvas while `EditorModel.showsStylePanel` is true, and it and the Layers panel take turns (`toggleStylePanel()`, `toggleLayers()`). ShotCore's `StylePanelLayout` says which sections it shows for the target. The screenshot's frame is ShotCore's `CanvasFrame` (padding in pixels and a `CanvasAspect`), which `EditorDocument.canvasRect` applies after the frame crop, so `contentRect`, and with it where the crop can go, never includes it. A gradient or Desktop fill is `EditorDocument.backgroundFill`, drawn by `AnnotationRenderer.drawFill` over `background`, which holds a colour close to it so shadows tint and the JPEG and checkerboard rules work unchanged. The desktop picture itself is `EditorDocument.desktopPicture`, which `DesktopPicture` reads off the main actor and the snapshot leaves out. Use for new captures saves the frame and fill as `CopiedStyle.canvas` only when there is one, and `applyNewCaptureStyle` puts them back. The video editor's clip inspector still uses `StylePopover.swift` until its own redesign.
