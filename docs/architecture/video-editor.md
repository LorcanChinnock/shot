# Video editor and GIF export

Read this before changing the timeline, trim, cuts, keyframes or GIF export.

- `VideoEditorModel` keeps its undo steps in ShotCore's `VideoEditHistory`, which also decides which changes join the step before them. What crop mode shows and applies, what stays selected after an undo and how a cut selection is marked are `Project` and `TimelineSelection` extensions in `VideoEditing+Selection.swift`.
- The timeline and composition logic is in ShotCore (`Timeline*`, `ProjectComposition`). So are keyframes, trim and cuts (`Keyframes`, `TrimRange`, and `CutList` in `VideoCuts.swift`).
- GIF export reads frames (`GIFExporter`, `GIFStreamWriter`) and encodes them (`GIFEncoder`, `ColorQuantizer`).
- Video goes through `AVVideoReader`, `AVVideoAssembler` and `AVVideoWriter`.
- The title bar (`VideoEditorTitleBar` in `VideoEditorView.swift`) follows the image editor's: the file and what's changed, read from ShotCore's `VideoEditSummary`, then Undo, Draw (`toggleDrawing()`), Inspector (`toggleInspector()`, also ⌥⌘I), Save or Save Copy, and Export, which opens and closes the Export panel (`toggleExportPanel()`). Export is the one main button until the panel opens or crop mode starts, when it steps back to secondary.
- The side panel right of the well and the controls area is `sidePanel`, a ShotCore `VideoSidePanel`: the Inspector or Export, which take turns through `VideoSidePanel.toggling`. Both start closed in every window.
- `InspectorPanel` (`InspectorPanel.swift`) shows the selection's keyable properties with their keyframe diamonds, the selected keyframe's easing, the one-click animations, the image editor's `StyleSection`s and a clip's crop, or the frame with nothing selected. ShotCore's `VideoInspectorPanel` decides the sections and maps each slider to a property and its label.
- `ExportPanel` (`ExportPanel.swift`) shows while `sidePanel` is `.export`. ShotCore's `VideoExportPanel` decides its groups for each format, the speed lock on a drawn MP4 and the length the export plays for. The panel refreshes `estimatedSize` only while it shows, and its foot shows `exportFraction`, the GIF's progress, with Cancel while anything exports. Each option change goes through `setOptions`, one undo step that `Preferences` remembers for Quick Access's Export GIF.
- While Draw is on, `DrawToolColumn` (in `AnnotationPalette.swift`) floats over the left of the video with the tools ShotCore's `VideoDrawTools` lists, and `VideoStyleBar` shows the image editor's style bar (`StyleBar`, through `StyleBarEditing`) at the top centre of the well, so drawing leaves the window's height alone. Crop video, or C while drawing, calls `cropFromDrawTools()`, which keeps the column up (`showsDrawTools`) and picks the tool again through `toolAfterCrop` when the crop is applied, cancelled or undone.
- Under the title bar, `VideoEditorRootView` puts the video in a well, sized and centred by ShotCore's `VideoWell` with room either side for the draw column, then the controls area: `PlaybackControls` (Play, the time, Split, Cut, Trim idle, Add media, Snap and zoom) over `TracksView`. The lanes always show, and the Video lane trims, cuts and splits. Each lane's name comes from `Project.laneName(ofTrack:)`, each cut's badge from `Project.mainCutMarks`, and the zoom slider maps through `TimelineZoom.sliderPosition(for:)`. `chromeHeight` counts the title bar, the well's insets and the controls area with a plain recording's ruler and Video lane (`PlaybackControls.areaHeight`, which includes `TracksView.baseHeight`); `tracksExtraHeight` adds the other lanes, and `fitWindowToTracks` grows the window by it. The well has a content shape as well as its clip, so a scaled-up clip's handles can't catch clicks meant for the title bar.
- Crop mode looks as the image editor's does. `VideoCropOverlay` washes what the crop leaves out in the well's colour and draws the crop's outline, thirds and marks from ShotCore's `CropMarks`, which `EditorCanvasView` also draws from, turned with the clip when it's a clip's crop. The bar is the image editor's `CropBarOverlay`, generic over `CropBarEditing`, which `VideoEditorModel` adopts in `VideoCropOverlay.swift`; it reports `cropBarClearance`, and `VideoWell.videoRect(showing:in:bottomClearance:)` fits the video above it. Return applies, Esc cancels and Delete resets (`resetCrop()`).
- `TransformOverlay` draws the selected clip's handles in `SelectionStyle`, as the annotation canvas does: a dashed accent outline, `SelectionHandle` squares on the corners and a round rotate handle on a stem. ShotCore's `TransformHandles.rotateHandle` puts that handle inside the outline's top when above would leave the well.
- Annotations on a video are drawn by the shared renderer; see [image-editor.md](image-editor.md).
