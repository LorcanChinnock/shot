## What Shot sends

Nothing. Shot collects no analytics, and the only time it connects to the network is to [check for updates](#updates), after you agree to it. The links in **Settings › About**, including Support Shot, open in your browser.

## Where your captures go

Captures go to the folder you choose in **Settings › General**, `~/Pictures/Shot` by default. When saving is off, they go to a temporary folder, and Shot deletes ones older than a day when it launches.

Text search, editor layers and video projects stay in Shot's own Application Support folder on your Mac, never inside the files you share.

## Permissions

Shot needs **Screen & System Audio Recording** access for every capture. **Accessibility** is only for capturing just the page of a browser window, to find the web page inside it. macOS asks for **Microphone** and **Camera** access only when Shot first uses them: when you record with them, or try them with **Test** or **Preview** in **Settings › Recording**.

To give Shot a permission before it needs it, click **Allow…** beside it in **Settings › About**. A permission you've allowed shows **Allowed**. Once macOS has asked and won't ask again, **Allow…** opens that list in System Settings.
