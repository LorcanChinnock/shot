AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT take a pasted screenshot, and your arrows and boxes show them where to look.

## Paste a marked-up screenshot

1. Take a screenshot and mark it up: circle the bug, add an arrow or a note.
2. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or any other assistant.

That's all most people need. Your marks show the assistant where to look.

> [!WARNING]
> Shot sends nothing anywhere. But whatever you paste goes to that assistant's provider, so blur or pixelate anything private first, as in [Annotate a screenshot](#annotate-a-screenshot).

## Give the assistant the file

Right-click a Quick Access card and choose **Copy Path**. An assistant that can open files, such as a coding tool running on your Mac, can read the image from its path. Copies are always the image alone.

The `shot://copy-path` link copies the newest capture's path without opening the card. See [Automation reference](#url-scheme).

## Paste the text instead

If the assistant only needs the words in a screenshot, use **Copy Text** to drag out an area and copy the text in it. Shot reads it on your Mac, so nothing leaves it until you paste.
