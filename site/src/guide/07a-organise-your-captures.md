The [gallery](#open-the-gallery) can keep captures in folders and tag them, for example by client, bug or project. Nothing changes until you make your first folder or tag. Taking a screenshot never asks you where it goes.

Folders are ordinary folders inside your save folder, and tags are Finder tags. Both show in Finder and Spotlight too, and they stay with a file when you move or rename it.

## Make a folder

1. Right-click a capture, or a selection of them, and choose **Move to › New Folder…**.
2. Type a name and click **Create and Move**.

A sidebar then appears on the left of the gallery, listing:

- **All**, which shows everything, in every folder.
- Your save folder itself, which shows only what isn't in a folder.
- Each of your folders.

Click **New Folder** at the bottom of the sidebar to make an empty one. Folders go one level deep: the gallery doesn't show folders inside folders.

## Move captures

- Drag thumbnails onto a folder in the sidebar. If the one you drag is selected, the whole selection moves.
- Or right-click and choose **Move to**, then a folder. Pick your save folder's name to take captures out of a folder.

If a folder already has a file with the same name, Shot leaves that capture where it was. Edits you can reopen stay reopenable after a move.

Dragging a thumbnail into a chat, an email or Finder still drops the file there, as before.

## Save new captures to a folder

Use the **Save new captures to** menu at the top of the sidebar to send new screenshots and recordings straight to a folder, for example while you work on one client. Pick your save folder's name to go back to normal. If you delete the folder in Finder, captures go to your save folder again.

## Tag captures

1. Right-click a capture, or a selection, and choose **Tag…**.
2. Type a tag and press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or click a tag you've used before. Click a ticked tag to take it off.

Tags named after Finder's colours, such as Red or Blue, show their colour.

## Filter by tag

Once a capture has a tag, a row of tags appears under the filters. Click a tag to show only the captures that have it. Click more tags to narrow it down further. Tags work together with Screenshots, Videos and GIFs, and with the folder you're in.

You can also type `tag:` and a tag's name in the search field, such as `tag:bug login`. Put quotes around a tag with a space in it: `tag:"Client A"`.
