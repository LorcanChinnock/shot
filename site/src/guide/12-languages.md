Shot is in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. It follows the language your Mac is set to.

## Use a different language for Shot

1. Open **System Settings › General › Language & Region**.
2. Under **Applications**, add Shot and choose its language.
3. Quit Shot and open it again.

> [!NOTE]
> File names stay in English.
