## Open Settings

Click Shot's menu bar icon and choose **Settings…**, press <kbd>⌘,</kbd> while a Shot window is in front, or click **Settings** at the foot of the [gallery](#find-your-captures)'s sidebar. To open a particular section, use [`shot://settings?section=recording`](#url-scheme), or any section name below.

Settings and the gallery share one Shot window. **‹ Library**, at the top of the Settings sidebar, goes back to your captures.

Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move between sections. **About**, at the foot of the sidebar, shows Shot's version and whether it's up to date.

## Find a setting

Type in the search field at the top right of the window, or press <kbd>⌘F</kbd>. Shot lists every setting whose name, description or a related word, such as "microphone" for **Input**, has the words you type, from all six sections, under each section's name, with their controls right there. Sections with no match fade in the sidebar. The same search finds your captures by name or by the text in them, listed after the settings.

Click **Show All** beside a section, or click a section in the sidebar, to clear the search and see the whole section. <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the search too.

## What each section controls

| Section | What you can change |
|---|---|
| General | Open Shot at login, the capture sound, cropping the menu bar off captures of the full screen, light or dark appearance (or following your Mac), the size of the text in Shot's own windows, panels and messages from the **Text size** menu (Default, Large or Larger, which leaves other apps alone), the save folder (**Show in Finder** opens it and **Choose…** picks another), the file name, PNG or JPEG, scaling Retina captures to 1×, and whether the editor's Save replaces the original |
| Capture | **After a capture:** saving, copying, Quick Access, or opening the editor instead. **Captured image:** the cursor, window shadows, and capturing only the page of a browser window. **While selecting:** the magnifier, the crosshair and the size label, with a preview that shows the ones you turn on. Move the pointer over the preview to drag an area and try them. Each of those three also has a key on the capture bar, <kbd>M</kbd>, <kbd>C</kbd> and <kbd>I</kbd>, for one capture |
| Quick Access | **Placement:** the screen corner and how long cards stay before they close. A preview shows a card in that corner, counts down its time and slides it away when the time runs out. Point at the card in the preview to see that the timer waits while the pointer is on it |
| Recording | **Sound:** the microphone, which input it uses (**Test** lights the level meter as you speak), and Mac sound, the sound from other apps. **Pointer:** the cursor, clicks and the region border. **After recording:** copying to the clipboard, and adjusting before every recording. **Camera:** the camera bubble, which camera and its size. Click **Preview** on the dark card above them to see your camera in the bubble's circle. **Quality:** the frame rate, and the GIF settings the video editor last used, which **Export GIF** on the Quick Access card follows |
| Shortcuts | **Use Shot for ⇧⌘4 and ⇧⌘5**, which takes the screenshot keys from macOS. **Global:** the screenshot and record shortcuts, shown as keys. Click one and press the new keys to change it. **Restore Defaults** at the top puts them back. **While picking what to capture** lists the keys that work while you choose an area, a window or a screen, such as <kbd>D</kbd> for a delay |
| About | The version and whether Shot is up to date, updates, links to this guide, the code and its licence, bug reports, the acknowledgements and sponsoring, each permission with **Allowed** or **Allow…**, the save folder, kept originals, and resetting all settings |

## Use dark mode

Shot's windows and panels follow your Mac's light or dark appearance. To pick one yourself:

1. Open **Settings › General**.
2. Next to **Appearance**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.

![Settings › General in dark mode, with Appearance set to Dark, the sidebar listing every section and the Behaviour and Files groups](../../../docs/media/settings-dark.png)

> [!NOTE]
> This changes Shot's own windows and panels only. Your captures look the same either way.

## Reset everything

In **Settings › About**, click **Reset…**, then **Click to confirm**. It restores every setting and shortcut. Your captures stay.
