## Open Settings

Click Shot's menu bar icon and choose **Settings…**, or press <kbd>⌘,</kbd> while a Shot window is in front. To open a particular section, use [`shot://settings?section=recording`](#url-scheme), or any section name below.

The [gallery](#find-your-captures) has its own window. **Open Gallery**, at the foot of the Settings sidebar, opens it.

Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move between sections. The foot of the sidebar shows Shot's version, or **Up to date** while Shot checks for updates by itself. Click it to open **About**.

## Find a setting

Type in the **Search** field at the top of the sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘F</kbd>. Shot lists every setting whose name or description has the words you type, from all six sections, under each section's name. Sections with no match fade in the sidebar.

Click **Show All** beside a section, or click a section in the sidebar, to clear the search and see the whole section. <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the search too.

## What each section controls

| Section | What you can change |
|---|---|
| General | Open Shot at login, the capture sound, cropping the menu bar off captures of the full screen, light or dark appearance (or following your Mac), the text size of Shot's own windows from the **Text size** menu (macOS's Accessibility › Text size doesn't reach other apps), the save folder (**Show in Finder** opens it and **Choose…** picks another), the file name, PNG or JPEG, scaling Retina captures to 1×, and whether the editor's Save replaces the original |
| Capture | What happens after a capture (save, copy, Quick Access, or the editor), the magnifier, the crosshair, the size label, the cursor, and window shadows |
| Quick Access | The screen corner and how long cards stay |
| Recording | Adjust before recording, frame rate, cursor, clicks, region outline, copying to the clipboard, the microphone and its input (with a **Test**), system audio, and the camera bubble's camera (with a **Preview**) and size. It also sums up the GIF settings the video editor last used. |
| Shortcuts | The macOS screenshot keys and every global shortcut |
| About | The version, updates, permissions, the save folder, links to the code and its licence, this guide, the acknowledgements, to report a bug and to sponsor Shot, and resetting all settings |

## Use dark mode

Shot's windows and panels follow your Mac's light or dark appearance. To pick one yourself:

1. Open **Settings › General**.
2. Next to **Appearance**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.

![Settings › General in dark mode, with Appearance set to Dark, the sidebar listing every section and the Behaviour and Files cards](../../../docs/media/settings-dark.png)

> [!NOTE]
> This changes Shot's own windows and panels only. Your captures look the same either way.

## Reset everything

In **Settings › About**, click **Reset**, then **Click to confirm**. It restores every setting and shortcut. Your captures stay.
