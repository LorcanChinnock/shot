# Gallery

![The gallery: an annotated screenshot, a GIF, a recording and three screenshots, with filters, sort and the thumbnail size slider](../../../docs/media/gallery.png)

**Open Gallery** in the menu bar menu (or [`shot://gallery`](#url-scheme)) shows every image, movie and GIF in your save folder, Shot's or not, kept up to date as you capture. It's the first section of the same window as Settings. Captures taken with saving turned off aren't in it.

- **Filter** by All, Screenshots, Videos or GIFs, **search** (⌘F), and **sort** by **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Name** or **Largest first**. Sorted by date, the gallery groups captures into Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month and then each older month. The slider (or ⌘+ and ⌘−) sets the thumbnail size.
- **Search** finds a file by its name, and a screenshot by the text in it too, ignoring case and accents. Shot reads the text on your Mac, at low priority in a separate process, a few seconds after a screenshot lands in the save folder. It stops while you record and doesn't start in Low Power Mode. The first time, it reads the screenshots already in the folder, newest first, which can take a few minutes for a big folder. What it reads stays in Shot's Application Support folder, never in your files. Recordings and GIFs are found by name only.
- **Select** with a click, ⌘-click, ⇧-click, ⌘A or the arrow keys, or tick the checkbox on a thumbnail to add it to the selection. **Select All** and **Deselect All** sit next to the count at the bottom.
- **Edit** (or Return) opens the selection in the [annotation editor](#annotation-editor) or [video editor](#video-editor), and asks first if that's more than 8 items. A GIF opens only if the video editor exported it, and then opens its [project](#video-editor). Double-click a screenshot, recording or GIF, or use the pencil or scissors button on a hovered screenshot or recording, to open just that one.
- **Quick Look** with Space.
- The buttons at the bottom **Edit**, **Copy**, **Show in Finder** or **Move to Trash** the selection. Right-click for the same, plus **Quick Look**. Right-click a single item and **Edit** reads **Annotate** or **Edit Video**, with **Rename…** too, **Export GIF** for a video, and **Copy Text** and [**Pin**](#quick-access) for a screenshot. Trashed files go to the Trash; ⌘Z puts them back, and so can Finder.
- Drag a thumbnail into another app.
- Anything an agent or the `shot` command made gets a small mark in its corner: a terminal for the command line, sparkles for an agent over [MCP](#the-mcp-server). Hover it to see who made it, what they did and when, such as **Made by Claude Code · annotate · 14:03**. A `shot` run always reads **Command line**, whoever started it. Once there's at least one, the filter adds **Made by agents**, which lists only those. Your own captures carry no mark.
